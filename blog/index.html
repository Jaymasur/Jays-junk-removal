PRACTICAL GUIDE

Scrapping smarter: simple ways to get more value from metal.

Scrapping can reduce disposal costs and keep reusable metal out of the landfill, but only when the sorting and extra driving are worth the return. A little preparation makes the trip safer, faster and more profitable.

01

Use a magnet first

If the magnet sticks, the item is usually steel or iron. If it does not, check whether it may be aluminum, copper, brass or stainless steel—materials that often pay more when separated.

02

Keep metals separated

Sort copper, brass, aluminum, stainless steel and regular steel into different containers. A mixed pile is faster to load, but clean separated material is easier for the yard to grade correctly.

03

Clean it only when it pays

Removing plastic, wood, rubber or steel attachments may increase the grade. Compare the extra value with the time it takes—an hour of labor is not always worth a few additional dollars.

04

Handle appliances safely

Never cut sealed refrigerator, freezer or air-conditioner lines. Refrigerants and oils require proper recovery. Call the scrap yard first to ask what appliances they accept and how each item must be prepared.

05

Call before making the trip

Prices, accepted materials, identification requirements and preparation rules can change. Ask the yard about current rates and restrictions before loading the truck or trailer.

06

Track weight and payout

Save the scale ticket and note the metal type, weight and amount paid. Over time, this shows which items and scrap yards are actually worth the fuel, labor and unloading time.

Safety first: Wear gloves, eye protection and sturdy footwear. Secure every load, watch for sharp edges, and never burn insulation from wire.
