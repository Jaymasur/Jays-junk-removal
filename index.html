Junk removal FAQ

Helpful answers based on verified business information.

How does junk removal pricing work?

Pricing is based on both volume and weight. The displayed trailer prices include up to the listed weight allowance for each tier.

How much fits in the trailer?

Each tier includes approximate household examples such as couches, chairs, mattresses, appliances, boxes, bags, and mixed clutter so you can visualize the load more easily.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Yes. Sending photos is encouraged because it helps Jay estimate how much trailer space the items may take up.

What items can you remove?

Current verified service information includes junk removal, furniture, appliances, general household clutter, construction debris, yard debris, and larger cleanout jobs.

What items can't you take?

Hazardous materials, flammable items, and restricted waste are not removed without special arrangements.

Do certain items cost extra?

They may. Final pricing can change based on actual weight, material type, specialty disposal, access, labor, and additional items. Existing confirmed surcharges include mattresses and tires.

Do you handle estate cleanouts?

Yes. Estate cleanouts, house cleanouts, garage cleanouts, basement cleanouts, and property cleanouts are part of the supporting cleanout services highlighted on the site.

What areas do you serve?

Jay's Junk Removal serves Wilkes-Barre, Luzerne County, Lackawanna County, Hanover Township, and surrounding Northeastern Pennsylvania communities within the service area.
