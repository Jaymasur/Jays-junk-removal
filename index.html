FAQ

Helpful answers based on the current verified business information.

How does junk removal pricing work?

Pricing is based on both volume and weight. The displayed trailer prices include up to the listed weight allowance for each tier.

What can the trailer tiers look like?

Each tier includes approximate household examples such as couches, chairs, mattresses, appliances, boxes, bags, and mixed clutter so you can visualize the space more easily.

Can I send pictures for an estimate?

Yes. Sending photos is encouraged because it helps Jay estimate how much trailer space the items may take up.

What areas do you service?

Jay's Junk Removal serves Luzerne County, Lackawanna County, Wilkes-Barre, Hanover Township, and surrounding communities in Northeastern Pennsylvania.

Are there additional disposal fees?

They may apply depending on material type, disposal requirements, specialty items, access, labor, and actual weight. Existing confirmed surcharges include mattresses and tires.

What types of jobs do you handle?

Current site content confirms junk removal, house cleanouts, yard cleanup, mulch work, and larger household cleanout jobs involving furniture and appliances.
