5 Steps to Declutter Your Home with Jay's Junk Removal

Step 1: Start Small—One Room at a Time

Don’t try to declutter the entire house in one day. Pick one space—like a garage, attic, or even just a junk drawer—and focus your energy there. Breaking the job into manageable areas makes it easier to stay on track and see progress.

Need help with garage cleanouts, basement junk, or bulky items? Jay’s Junk Removal handles all types of residential cleanups.

Step 2: Sort and Separate

Create three piles: Keep, Donate/Sell, and Toss. Be honest—if you haven’t used it in a year and it doesn’t bring you joy, it’s probably time to let it go.

Think about that pile of old shirts you haven’t worn in forever, the oversized bean bag in the corner no one uses, or the stack of mismatched mugs in the back of your cabinet. Those items might be better off donated—or ready to go.

And don’t forget about old appliances! That rusty microwave or broken mini fridge taking up space in the basement? Load it up and take it to the junk yard. You might even get a few bucks for the metal—enough to treat yourself to lunch after a long day of cleaning.

Jay’s Junk Removal can help haul away all of it, from clothing to appliances. We recycle what we can and make disposal easy so you don’t have to lift a finger.

Step 3: Schedule a Pick-Up for the Big Stuff

Old furniture, broken appliances, yard debris, or piles of junk can be a pain to move on your own. Don’t risk injury or waste your weekend—call in the pros.

Step 4: Deep Clean and Refresh

Once the clutter is gone, it’s time to freshen things up. Wipe down surfaces, vacuum the floors, open the windows, and let the spring air in. You’ll be amazed at how much lighter and more comfortable your home feels once it’s clean and clear.

We’ll clear the junk—so you can enjoy the peace.

Step 5: Tackle the Little Things Before They Pile Up

Clutter doesn’t show up overnight—it builds slowly. Take 10 minutes each week to stay ahead of it. Toss old mail, return items to their place, or clean out one drawer at a time. These small habits go a long way toward keeping your home clear year-round.

And if the clutter ever gets away from you, Jay’s Junk Removal is just a phone call away.

Final Thoughts

Decluttering doesn’t have to be overwhelming. With a little planning—and a little help—you can reclaim your space and start the season fresh.
